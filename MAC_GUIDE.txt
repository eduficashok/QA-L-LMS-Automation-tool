========================================================================
   LMS QA Automation Tool - Quick Start Guide for macOS (MacBook)
========================================================================

HOW TO RUN ON YOUR MAC (Takes 10 seconds):
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. UNZIP the folder to your Desktop or Downloads.

2. DOUBLE-CLICK this file:
   👉 start_mac.command

3. THAT'S IT! 
   - A Safari/Chrome browser window will automatically open with the 
     LMS QA Dashboard at: http://127.0.0.1:8080
   - (First time only: It will take ~1 minute to automatically set up 
     the browser engine in the background).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO SCAN A COURSE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Select "Canvas QC Scanner" or "Blackboard Ultra Inventory".
2. Paste the Course URL.
3. Click "Start Automated QC Scan".
4. If your University SSO login appears, log in once and click 
   "Confirm Login & Continue" on the dashboard.
5. View the complete HTML report with broken images, broken links, 
   and screenshots directly in your browser!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE FOR FIRST-TIME RUN:
If macOS says "Cannot open start_mac.command because it is from an 
unidentified developer":
1. Right-click (or Control+Click) on start_mac.command
2. Click "Open"
3. Click "Open" again in the popup.
